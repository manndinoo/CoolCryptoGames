# Lineman checklists: installed in the AI prompt (server/ai/policy.mjs) as of Linewatch 2.0

Use all applicable categories: tangent + transformer and dead-end + transformer are valid combinations. Every item records component, result, evidence/photo IDs, observations vs suspicion, urgency rationale and follow-up. Result choices: no visible concern; defect observed; suspected/field verification needed; not visible/not inspected; not applicable with reason.

## Tangent poles — owner's required checks
- [ ] Loose bolts/hardware, missing/displaced/damaged hardware, washers sinking into wood, bolts into rotten wood.
- [ ] Loose arms; split/rotting arms; sagging, twisting, strain/alignment; braces/attachments.
- [ ] Bent or loose arm pins and their attachment points.
- [ ] Tie wire quality: breaks, wear, missing/displaced ties and conductor seating.
- [ ] Wire within/near ties on insulators: broken strands, abrasion, deformation and burnt wire.
- [ ] Tracking/electrical distress.
- [ ] Connected pole ground: visible continuity, broken/disconnected portions and damaged connections/protection.
- [ ] Wire clearances for known kV/configuration, actual measurements and applicable utility criteria.
- [ ] Cracked/broken insulators, missing pieces, displacement and abnormal strain.
- [ ] Rotten pole at groundline and visible deterioration/damage elsewhere.
- [ ] Pole plumb/apparent lean with perspective limits.
- [ ] Automatics/splices: location, body condition, movement, damaged strands and heat evidence. Presence alone is not a defect.

## Dead-end poles — owner's required checks
- [ ] Broken/worn poly insulators: sheds, exposed core, end fittings, tracking and alignment.
- [ ] Loose/broken shoes, grips and attachment hardware.
- [ ] Birdcaging/broken/abraded/burnt wire near the shoe.
- [ ] Tracking/flashover damage.
- [ ] Leaning/displaced pole, alignment associated with conductor pull.
- [ ] Broken/slack/damaged/corroded down guys and attachments.
- [ ] Guy guards missing/damaged/displaced where required.
- [ ] Bolts pulling through wood, enlarged holes, crushing/splitting at attachments.
- [ ] Split/separating/decayed pole tops at loaded attachments.
- [ ] Rotten/damaged poles and groundline.

## Transformer poles — owner's required checks
- [ ] Leaking transformer: source, oil trails, extent and current condition.
- [ ] Arcing/tracking/burning, distinguish active events from old stains.
- [ ] Broken/disconnected neutrals and bonds as required by equipment configuration.
- [ ] Untaped/uninsulated hot legs/secondary leads where design requires coverings/insulation.
- [ ] Broken/burnt/cracked/deteriorated arresters and lead/ground connections.
- [ ] Cracked/burnt services, insulation abrasion and damaged connections.
- [ ] Services touching/rubbing poles or equipment, accounting for intended design.
- [ ] Loose cutouts, damaged insulation, mounting/attachment condition.
- [ ] Wildlife guards: required presence, damage, displacement and incomplete coverage.
- [ ] Equipment spacing/contact concerns: bushings, leads, cutouts, arresters, tank and other equipment.
- [ ] Loose-looking/separated/damaged connections, corrosion and heating evidence.
- [ ] Rust/missing paint and physical tank damage; cosmetic vs material deterioration.
- [ ] Bird/squirrel nests and debris interfering with equipment or creating supported contact/ignition danger.

## Additional applicable items
- [ ] Woodpecker/insect damage, cavities, impact/fire damage and deterioration at holes/attachments.
- [ ] Erosion/washout, excavation, exposed foundations, settlement and pole movement.
- [ ] Anchor displacement, rods, clamps/retaining hardware and guy strain insulators.
- [ ] Conductor damage along spans, abnormal-looking sag, rubbing/contact, jumpers and suspension hardware.
- [ ] Transformer bushings: cracks, damage/contamination; brackets/bands and supporting wood.
- [ ] Communication attachments, risers/conduits and foreign attachments causing interference/damage.
- [ ] Vegetation contact, damaged trees threatening spans and fuels associated with supported ignition mechanisms.
- [ ] Pole/equipment IDs, previous defects, repairs and worsening unresolved conditions.

## Photo limits
Seated bolts do not prove tightness. Visible ground/neutral connections do not prove electrical continuity. Solid-looking wood does not rule out internal/below-ground decay. Bare hardware may be intentional. Clearance requires configuration/measurement, not kV alone. Phone plumb guides show projected alignment, not capacity. No checklist item name automatically implies Critical; apply the separate grading policy.
