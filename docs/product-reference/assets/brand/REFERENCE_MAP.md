# Brand Reference Map

## Approved direction

### 04-approved-hybrid-desktop.png

Primary desktop reference. Use its dark graphite shell, cobalt structure, bone-white type, restrained glass depth, acid-yellow actions, game-card prominence, tournament module, and overall information hierarchy.

Do not copy its fake counts, chain logos, game names, earnings copy, or exact generated logo typography.

### 05-approved-hybrid-mobile.png

Primary mobile reference. Use its compact header, hero composition, large Play action, trending strip, horizontal game cards, tournament module, and bottom navigation.

Do not render the entire page at once on an actual phone. The real experience scrolls naturally and respects safe areas.

## Logo instruction

The boards contain generated logo concepts, not final production vectors. Build a simple original SVG system:

- `CCG` wordmark
- Three-tile monogram
- One-color mark
- Dark-background mark
- Light-background mark
- Favicon

The monogram should remain legible at 16 pixels, must not resemble a coin, casino chip, blockchain logo, or esports shield, and must not depend on glow for recognition.
